Ask your supervisor for a decision you can't make yourself. It goes to your supervisor, never to the user directly. Your task shows as waiting, and the app won't treat you as stuck while it waits. The answer arrives as a message from your supervisor; carry on meanwhile with anything that doesn't depend on it, or end your turn.
- question: what needs deciding, with the context needed to decide it, in a few sentences.
- options: the choices you see, each a few words (optional, at most 9).
