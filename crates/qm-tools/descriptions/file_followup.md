File a follow-up for work outside your task: a bug you found, a stale doc, a failure that already exists on main. It goes to your supervisor as a proposal and doesn't start by itself. Carry on with your own task.
- title: a conventional-commit summary, lowercase imperative, at most 72 characters: "fix(upload): retry on slow tmp".
- goal: what done looks like, concrete enough to check, with what you saw.
- kind: build, fix, plan, research, lookup, teach, review, reproduce or unclear.
- project: a registered project's name, if not your own (optional).
