Ask the user a question, shown as a question card. Their answer arrives as their next message, so end your turn after asking. Ask one question at a time, and only when you need a decision you can't make yourself: a question rings the user's bell. Never for a plan's questions: those are on the plan's page.
- question: the question, in Markdown. Must not be empty.
- options: up to 9 short answers the user can pick with one click, each at most 60 characters (optional; they can always type their own).
