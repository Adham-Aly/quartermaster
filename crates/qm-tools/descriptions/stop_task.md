Stop one of your tasks' work and retire it: its worktree and branch are removed, so uncommitted changes and unmerged commits are dropped. A plain-folder task's edits stay where they are, since nothing is undone. The task stays open so you can close it or start a follow-up. Its report stays available to a task that builds on it.

- reason: why, in a few words (optional).
