Schedule a wake for later: at that time you get a turn with the note as an app event. If the app is closed then, it fires on the next launch, marked late.
- due: when, as an RFC 3339 time with an offset, e.g. 2026-10-19T09:00:00-04:00. Must be in the future; the context block has the current time and the user's time zone.
- what: what to do then, in plain words.
