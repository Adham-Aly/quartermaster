Push your branch and open or update its pull request. Direct-PR projects only. Refused while the task is held, before a required review passes, and when a check is failing on your last push and you haven't declared a diagnosis for it with ci_diagnosis. Your commit names and the title are checked: type(scope): summary, lowercase imperative, at most 72 characters, and no Co-Authored-By line naming a model. The app then polls the checks on your new head every 30 seconds and wakes you once with the result.
- title: the pull request title, in the same form as a commit name.
- body: an ordinary pull request description with a Summary and a Testing heading (required at the standard and reviewed levels; Testing names the exact commands and tests you ran and what they showed).
Write the title, body and commit messages in your own words for the project's reviewers: never mention task IDs (T-0042) or the app's internal terms.
