Report your plan. The app checks $QM_PLAN_DIR/plan.md as plan_check does, then wakes your supervisor, which starts the build from it.
- outcome: done when the plan is complete; blocked when something you can't fix stops you (say what in blockers); needs_decision only when you can't go on without a choice nobody else can make (you wait for the answer).
- answer: a short summary for your supervisor: what the plan builds and the main decisions. Never the plan itself; the plan is plan.md.
- blockers: what stops you.
