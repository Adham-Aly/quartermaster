Ask your supervisor for a decision only when you truly can't go on without it: a step that would lose data, spend money, reach outside the project, break a stated constraint, or a contradiction in what was asked. Everything else is yours to decide: choose the approach you'd recommend and record it under "Decisions made without the user" in plan.md.
It goes to your supervisor, never to the user directly; it decides, and asks the user only if it can't. Your task shows as waiting; the answer arrives as a message from your supervisor.
- question: what needs deciding, with the context needed to decide it, in a few sentences.
- options: the choices you see, each a few words (optional, at most 9).
